How this was made · sources, geography and limits

What a number means

Every value is an area-weighted mean of lwe_thickness in the CSR GRACE/GRACE-FO RL06.3 all-corrections file, created 24 August 2026 and retrieved 29 September 2026. Anomalies use the source’s 2004.000–2009.999 mean baseline. A negative value means less than the baseline, not negative water. Zero does not mean empty.

The file reports centimetres of equivalent water thickness using 1,025 kg/m³. We retain that convention throughout, including hypothetical components. The layer is a way to express mass per area, not water-table depth or remaining supply. No conversion to freshwater volume or total available water is made.

Where the regions are

These are author-defined coordinate windows, not whole drainage basins or countries. The Northern India window also includes neighboring areas. Display cells whose centres lie in a window are intersected with CSR’s binary v02 land mask. All edges align with 0.25° cell boundaries. The map silhouette also comes from that mask.

Weights are exact cell surface areas between parallels and meridians on an ellipsoid with the specified 6,378,136.3 m semi-major axis and WGS84 flattening (1/298.257223563, following the file’s WGS84 grid label). Coastal ocean cells are excluded. The supplied correction does not remove all coastal leakage or boundary uncertainty.

What is corrected

CSR applies degree-1 geocentre corrections, satellite-laser-ranging C20 replacements, C30 replacements for GRACE-FO, and removes ICE6G-D glacial isostatic adjustment. The monthly GAD atmosphere/ocean de-aliasing field is restored to ocean pixels only; those pixels are excluded here. Ellipsoidal corrections are already applied separately for land and ocean. No extra filtering, smoothing, or gain factors are applied. JPL’s optional scaling rules do not apply to this CSR product.

Time and missingness

The file contains 258 solutions in 291 calendar slots, April 2002–June 2026. The 33 absent nominal months are blank. July 2017–May 2018 is the 11-month interval between the last GRACE and first GRACE-FO solution. Lines stop at gaps; they never interpolate across them.

“Monthly” is approximate. Nominal labels for records 1–257 come from the official PO.DAAC record-index table; November 2011 and May 2015 have midpoints in the preceding month. The latest June 2026 label comes from the file’s bounds because the days table currently stops at May. The inspector retains exact midpoints, outer time bounds, and listed unused days. Bounds do not imply every day was used. End bounds are exclusive; days-table end dates are inclusive.

How much can we infer?

CSR’s 0.25° display cells represent roughly 1° equal-area native mascons. Effective GRACE resolution is roughly 300 km; neighboring estimates are not independent. CSR cautions against regions below about 200,000 km² and against single-grid-point analysis. Our smallest window exceeds 1.1 million km². The patch shows identities, not local water measurements; its native boundaries are still not an independence guarantee.

Over land, interpreting the residual mass as water assumes hydrology dominates; other solid-Earth processes can also contribute. The selected file supplies no uncertainty or covariance array. We do not invent an error band by treating pixels as independent observations. Regularization, leakage, correction-model errors and changing instrument performance remain. CSR notes accelerometer transplants for late GRACE and GRACE-FO. The figures are estimates, and later releases can revise them.

The component controls are a bookkeeping experiment with three chosen values and one balancing value. All are signed anomalies relative to hypothetical component baselines. They show that the regional sum alone cannot identify groundwater, soil, snow/ice or surface-water change. They are not observed decompositions, a full gravity forward model, or a claim that every allocation is hydrologically possible. Well, snow, soil and surface-water measurements or models would add constraints. We include none, and make no pumping attribution.
