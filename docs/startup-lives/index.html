03 / WHAT THIS CAN TELL US

A complete little cohort.
An incomplete view of success.

This is every venture in the published roster of YC’s first summer: Reddit, Infogami, Kiko, Loopt, TextPayMe (originally Firecrawl), ClickFacts, Memamp and Simmery Axe. Graham’s 2007 account independently gives the total as eight. Later ventures stay outside the denominator.

The lesson is about definitions and continuity, not a recipe for winning. A small, selected accelerator batch is not a sample of all startups. Shared founders, networks and a merger also mean these are not eight independent experiments.

Twenty-one years of follow-up helps, but does not solve missing histories. Prominent companies leave richer records. We have no comparable founder or investor returns ledger; no control group tells us what YC, a pivot or a particular decision caused. “Why” is attributed to the speaker, or left unknown.

Our seven final path labels are editorial classifications: public, acquired, assets sold, merged, stopped, operating privately, and unresolved. They do not stand in for legal dissolution, profitability or investment success. No survival curve is estimated. The time control reveals dated milestones; it does not reconstruct what was publicly known at that earlier date.

Selection, dates & missing evidence

Observation cut: 29 September 2026. A retrieval date is not an operating date. Reddit has a July 2026 company report; ClickFacts’ last dated operating report used here is August 2012. Empty years are not newly observed failures.

Some events have exact dates; others only a year or a retrospective approximate date. Points with only year precision sit within that year for legibility. Simmery’s CV supplies employment dates, not its legal closure. Loopt’s retirement was announced without an exact date, so we show a plan, not a fabricated shutdown day.

The historical 2005 Firecrawl name belongs to the TextPayMe team; it is unrelated to today’s company with that name. Infogami is counted separately at entry, then joined to Reddit’s path. Memamp’s founder later working at Reddit is not a merger.

Membership is reconciled against a historical roster, not an undisclosed acceptance ledger. We chose depth across one complete named batch over a larger, less verifiable sample. The YC directory covers publicly launched companies and its terms prohibit scraping; it was not exported for this piece.
