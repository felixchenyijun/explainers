    How to read this graph

    AI has many frontiers that move at different rates. More capability can expose a limit in verification, resources, recovery or institutions. This graph connects those limits across 32 stack topics and 16 broad knowledge families.

    Every node has an advance and an unresolved gate. Zoom to read both; select a topic for its evidence, limits and connections. Solid arrows run from a dependency toward what it enables or constrains. Dashed arrows carry feedback.

    Follow a trace to highlight a continuous route. Use the cause filter to find the same mechanism in distant parts of the system. Hover or select a topic to expose its local connections. Click a line for the reasoning behind that link.

    Positions, node sizes, line lengths and spacing are editorial layout choices. They do not measure capability or maturity. Connections are analytical synthesis supported by their endpoint evidence, not experimentally estimated causal effects. Coverage is broad, not every human subdiscipline.

    The original HBS jagged-frontier study concerns uneven performance across tasks. Extending that idea to infrastructure and institutions is the synthesis in this graph. Older studies explain mechanisms; they are not current model rankings.

    Controls: drag to pan; scroll or pinch to zoom; + / − to zoom; 0 to fit; Escape to close a detail. The overview map also moves the camera. All topics and controls are keyboard accessible.
