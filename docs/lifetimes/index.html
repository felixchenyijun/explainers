Higher / lower = one standard deviation above / below average satisfaction. The dashed slope is not statistically distinguishable from zero; it is not proof of no relationship.

The quality of the connection mattered. Typical time spent with others did not show this buffering pattern. The study points toward how a relationship feels, beyond how many hours it occupies.

Limitation: 1–5; happiness: 1–7. These lines re-express published slopes while holding previous-day happiness fixed. They are fitted associations, not participant diaries or a forecast for changing someone’s marriage.

Waldinger & Schulz (2010), simple slopes. Participants averaged 7.6 of eight interviews. All were white; participating couples were healthy enough to complete the assessment. Reverse influence and unmeasured factors remain possible.

05 / Back to the human scale

Who can we
lean on?

Even the people available to lean on can change. A 2024 analysis of 235 men estimated that emotional support networks shrank from about two providers at age 30 to one at age 90.

A growth-curve result, not a universal trajectory or a count of all friends. Petrova and colleagues (2024). The archival US male sample limits generalization. No values between these endpoints have been invented here.

The thread running through these findings is the quality and availability of connection. Return to Henry and Rosa’s table: a whole life contains more than a study can measure, and a relationship contains more than time spent together.

What the graphics represent

The timeline contains only four dated events. Blank years mean no event represented from this excerpt, not that nothing happened. Illustrations are symbolic. Cohort dots encode counts. Regrouping illustrates totals, not tracked identities; partnered-group cohort composition is not shown. We do not match the book’s pseudonyms to any paper’s sample.

The selection graphic is not a mortality estimate. The original denominator is 724; the partnered-group denominator is 201. The three-stage graphic shows measurement windows, not yearly monitoring of the same variable.
