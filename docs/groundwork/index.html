This representative house uses concrete strip footings, an enclosed crawlspace, platform framing and a ventilated attic. Construction methods, trade sequencing, inspections and enclosure details vary by site, climate and local requirements. This is an educational model, not a set of construction drawings. Geometry and time are condensed, and the animated systems are schematic.
